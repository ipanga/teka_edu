## Product Decisions

- Teka Edu is a parent-led after-school reinforcement platform — a digital répétiteur guided by
  the parent (ADR-039).
- The year's pacing is authored before a month's lessons (ADR-040).
- French Cycle 1 is the curriculum; the DRC PNEM is a compatibility and enrichment reference
  (ADR-037).

## What the next task should know

- **Do not regenerate September.** It is authored, validated and deployed. The 35-minute days are
  valid under the 30–45 policy.
- October has not been started, and should not be until September has been reviewed by a person.
- The content emitter used to produce September was a one-off script kept outside the repository;
  the JSON in `content/` is canonical and hand-editable. Re-authoring a month means writing
  content, not re-running a generator.
- `tools/annual-plan/build.ts` **is** committed and does regenerate the annual plan; edit it
  rather than the generated JSON.
