# September illustration briefs — not accepted replacements

These briefs apply the independent audit, not the previous acceptance judgments. All replacements remain outside the registry until the complete audit and in-app review are ready. The canonical lesson text is binding.

## Shared direction

Painted early-childhood educational illustration: soft, rounded organic contours, gentle gouache-like colour areas, muted warm palette and minimal fine contour. No photorealism, heavy black icon stroke, anatomy assembled from primitive shapes, embedded text, logos, watermark or external dependencies. Recognition images have a plain ivory background, one subject filling roughly 80% of the square, readable at 128 px. Story images use only approved characters/actions and limited environmental context. Preserve exact counts and relationships.

## Candidate hand

`astra-drafts/corps-main-candidate.png` was made using the built-in imagegen tool. Draft 1 was rejected as too realistic; draft 2 was rejected because fingertip highlights resembled nails on the palm. Draft 3 removes those patches and has five connected digits. It is a local candidate, NOT registered or approved. Further phone/TV comparison and consistency review are required.
