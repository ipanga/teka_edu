# Testing a real session

How to run one September lesson with your child and tell us what happened. No technical knowledge
is needed, and it takes about 45 minutes including the notes.

This answers **usability** questions — can a parent run this? — and nothing else. It is not
pedagogical review (see the end).

---

## 1. Open the app

On your phone or tablet:

**https://teka-edu-staging.vercel.app**

Add it to your home screen if you want to find it again easily.

The first screen asks which class. Tap **3ème maternelle** — it is the only one with lessons for
now, and the other two say so rather than pretending.

## 2. Choose the day

Tap **« Voir toutes les séances de septembre »**, then pick **séance 1** (mardi 1er septembre) for
the first test — it is the start of the programme and assumes nothing.

If you would rather test a fuller day, **séance 3** has a story, the shapes and a dance;
**séance 11** has counting and the composition of numbers.

## 3. Prepare

The **À préparer** screen lists what to fetch. It is short on purpose. If you are missing
something, tap **« Je n'ai pas tout : que puis-je utiliser ? »** — there is a replacement for
everything.

Then tap **Commencer la leçon**.

During an activity you can tap **« Montrer à l'enfant »** to hand the device over: the child's
part fills the screen and nothing else on the page can be tapped, so a stray finger cannot end
the lesson. **« Revenir au guide du parent »** brings you back.

## 4. Run it as you normally would

- Read the sentence in **« La part de l'enfant »** aloud, exactly as written.
- Tap **« Montrer à l'enfant »** when there is something to look at or touch; the screen becomes
  the child's.
- Tap **« Afficher le conseil au parent »** whenever you are unsure what to do. Using it often is
  useful information, not a failure.
- When the screen says **« Posez l'écran »**, put it down and do the activity.
- Tap **« Terminé »** or **« Suivant »** when you are ready to move on — you decide, not the app.

**Do not do anything differently because it is a test.** If an instruction is unclear, hesitate
the way you really would. If your child loses interest, let them. If you want to stop, tap
**« Terminer pour aujourd'hui »** — stopping early is a result, not a broken test.

## 5. Tell us what happened

At the end, tap **« Noter comment ça s'est passé »**. The form asks about the session, never about
your child.

These are the ones that matter most:

1. **How long did it really take?**
2. **Was any instruction unclear to you?**
3. **Was any instruction unclear to your child?**
4. **Where did your child lose interest, or get tired?**
5. **Did you need the English help?**
6. **Were the pictures big enough, and did they help?**
7. **Did the pictures help — and where was one missing?**
8. **Did tapping the screen help, or interrupt?**
9. **Was anything too easy or too hard?**
10. **Did your child want to keep going at the end?**
11. **Anything you noticed that we did not ask about** — that is often the most useful line.

Press **« Enregistrer dans ce navigateur »**, then copy the text in the **À copier** box and send
it back however is convenient.

## 6. Privacy

The form asks nothing about your child: no name, no age, no school. Everything stays in your
browser until you copy it out yourself. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account.

Please do not type your child's name into the free notes either.

---

## What this is not

**Usability tested ≠ pedagogically reviewed.**

A real session tells us whether a parent can run the lesson and whether a child stays with it. It
does not tell us whether the teaching is right for a five-year-old: that is decided by the
pedagogical review gate, reading a generated package against the official programme (ADR-047).
The two answer different questions, and neither stands in for the other. September has been
corrected after its first review but has not completed the gate, so all 88 lessons are still
marked `review` and none is `approved`.

Both things are needed. They are not the same thing, and one never substitutes for the other.
